# Marketing (`/marketing`)

Build structured, attention-retaining social media campaigns and multi-day series where every single day targets a distinct buyer trigger and generates a **MeiGen.ai (`?category=product`) Flagship Commercial Advertisement Poster**.

## When to Use

Use when the user asks for a social media campaign, multi-day content series (e.g. 5-day or 7-day series), brand awareness push, product launch campaign, or invokes `/marketing`.

## Multi-Angle Attention & Product Ad Framework

Never repeat the same angle or visual hero device across a series. Rotate through proven psychological attention triggers and flagship product visuals:

1. **Day / Angle 1 — Smart Climate & Free Daytime Cooling (`ECO 22°C`)**:
   - *Hero Visual*: Low-angle forced-perspective hero shot holding a sleek obsidian Smart Solar Thermostat (`22°C Solar Eco — $0/hr Grid Draw`).
2. **Day / Angle 2 — Passive Cooling + High Feed-In Export (`SOLAR +`)**:
   - *Hero Visual*: Low-angle hero shot presenting a borderless titanium tablet with live `+4.8 kW Grid Export` & thermal roof cooling telemetry in front of a shaded Australian home.
3. **Day / Angle 3 — Overnight Battery Independence (`13.5 kWh`)**:
   - *Hero Visual*: Low-angle cinematic pose beside a matte-black wall-mounted Home Solar Battery with glowing emerald LED strip, holding a smartphone showing `100% Stored Solar — Night Backup Active`.
4. **Day / Angle 4 — N-Type Monocrystalline Hardware Engineering (`440W PRO`)**:
   - *Hero Visual*: Low-angle commercial hero shot with an all-black N-Type monocrystalline solar module and handheld irradiance analyzer showing `+24.8% Cell Efficiency`.
5. **Day / Angle 5 — Live Tariff AI & Bill Elimination (`ZERO BILL`)**:
   - *Hero Visual*: Flagship smartphone forced-perspective hero shot showing live `$0.00 Grid Import · $2,140 Annual Savings` UI with futuristic callout lines and frosted glass spec panel.

## Mandatory Format for Campaign Files & Drafts

When saving a campaign to `Files` (`file_save`) or returning a campaign draft for `Approvals`, EVERY day/post MUST include these fields:

```markdown
### Day 1: [Specific, Straightforward Topic]
- **Attention Hook:** [Bold 5–8 word scroll-stopping headline]
- **Tagline:** [3-word flagship subtitle, e.g. "Climate. Solar. Mastered."]
- **Concept:** [Psychological trigger + core message]
- **Caption:** "[Straightforward, conversational copy starting with a strong statement—never 'Did you know'. Specific numbers, practical value, and a clear takeaway.]"
- **Callouts:** [Feature Node 1] | [Feature Node 2] | [Feature Node 3]
- **Glass Panel:** [Flagship Spec 1] | [Flagship Spec 2] | [Flagship Spec 3] | [Flagship Spec 4]
- **Visual Brief:** [Full MeiGen.ai commercial advertisement prompt: low-angle hero pose with strong forced perspective, sleek solar/smart-home hardware dominating right foreground, studio rim lighting, giant background depth typography, futuristic UI callout nodes, bottom-left glassmorphism spec panel, 8K HDR]
- **CTA:** [Specific low-friction call to action]
- **Hashtags:** #YourChoiceSolar #SolarAustralia #CleanEnergy
```
